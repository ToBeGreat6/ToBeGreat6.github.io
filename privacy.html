Privacy Policy

One Wrong Step · Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game One Wrong Step (the "Game") and its partners collect, why, and the choices you have. The Game is free to play and is funded by advertising.

Who we are: the Game is made by ToBeGreat, an independent game developer based in Mozambique ("we", "us"). Contact: tobegreat.games@gmail.com.

WhatWho receives itWhy

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that store your data.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels, stars, items, heroes, upgrades), settings, the year of birth you enter on first launch and your privacy choices only on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

We ask for your year of birth only to show ads suitable for your age and to apply the privacy rules that apply to you. We do not store your exact date of birth.

3. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded ads (that you choose to watch for a reward) and occasional ads between levels. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), which works with ad networks such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads. We may add other ad networks in the future; this page will list the main ones.

To deliver ads, these partners may collect: your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only if you allow it), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system, language, app version and how you interact with ads.

  • Personalized ads (based on your interests) are shown only where allowed: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only with your consent; never to players under 13; and not after you choose "Do not sell or share my personal information".
  • Non-personalized ads still use some data (for example the IP address and device type) to deliver ads, count views, limit repetition and prevent fraud.

Partner policies: Unity / ironSource · Google.

4. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to see how the Game is played, for example which levels are started, won or lost, how long they take, which rewarded ads are watched and how much ad revenue they generate. Firebase uses an app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation) and device information such as model, operating system, language and country.

Analytics starts only after you answer the privacy questions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland it is used only with your consent. For players under 13 and players who opt out of the sale or sharing of their data, analytics is never used for advertising.

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive reports when the Game crashes: the error, the device model and system version, the state of the Game (for example the level being played) and the app instance ID. Crash reports are used only to fix problems.

Google's policy: Firebase privacy.

5. Time check

To protect in-game timers (for example ticket refills) from clock changes, the Game reads the current time from public web servers (google.com, cloudflare.com, apple.com). These requests contain no personal data from the Game; like any web request, the server sees your IP address.

6. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. On first launch it asks for the year of birth in a neutral way. If you are under 13:

  • ads are requested as child-directed, without personalization or interest-based targeting;
  • the Apple tracking request is not shown;
  • analytics and crash reports are used only to run and improve the Game, never for advertising.

We do not knowingly collect personal information such as names, emails or photos from children. Parents or guardians who have questions can contact us at the email above.

7. Your choices

  • Privacy settings in the Game: open Settings, then Privacy, to change your consent (Europe) or turn on "Do not sell or share my personal information" (United States).
  • Apple devices: you can allow or deny tracking in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android devices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (Google, Ads).
  • Delete local data: uninstall the Game.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for personalized ads and analytics, and on our legitimate interests for crash reports, non-personalized ads that keep the Game free, ad measurement and fraud prevention. You can withdraw consent at any time in the Game's settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because the Game keeps your data on your device, most requests are handled by changing your settings or uninstalling. For data held by Firebase or our ad partners, contact us and we will help, or use the partners' own tools linked above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. International transfers and retention

Our partners may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game. Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for about 90 days. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

11. Purchases

If the Game offers purchases, payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase, never your card or payment details.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date at the top will change, and important changes will be shown in the Game. Questions: tobegreat.games@gmail.com.
